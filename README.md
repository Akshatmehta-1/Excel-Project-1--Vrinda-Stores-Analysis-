Annual Sales Report 2022 - Vrinda Stores

📌 Overview

Vrinda Stores aims to analyze its annual sales data for 2022 to gain insights into customer behavior and optimize sales strategies for 2023. This project focuses on answering key business questions through data analysis and visualization using Excel.

🔍 Key Questions Solved

📊 Comparison of Sales and Orders - A single chart visualizing the trend.

🏆 Month with Highest Sales and Orders - Identifying peak sales periods.

👥 Customer Demographics - Understanding whether men or women purchased more.

📦 Order Status Analysis - Categorizing different order statuses in 2022.

🗺 Top 10 States Contributing to Sales - Identifying the most profitable regions.

🎯 Age and Gender Relationship - Analyzing purchase patterns based on demographics.

🛍 Sales Channel Contribution - Determining which channel drove maximum revenue.

📈 Highest Selling Category - Identifying the best-performing product category.

🔄 Project Steps

1️⃣ Data Cleaning - Handling missing values, removing duplicates, and ensuring data consistency.

2️⃣ Data Processing - Transforming and structuring data for analysis.

3️⃣ Data Analysis - Performing statistical and trend analysis.

4️⃣ Interactive Dashboard - Creating a user-friendly dashboard for visualization.

5️⃣ Insights - Deriving business insights and recommendations for future growth.

🛠 Tools Used

🏢 Microsoft Excel (for data cleaning, processing, analysis, and visualization)

💡 Insights & Business Recommendations

✔ Identified peak sales months to optimize marketing strategies.

✔ Recognized top-performing states and categories to focus inventory planning.

✔ Analyzed customer demographics to personalize marketing campaigns.

✔ Evaluated order statuses to improve logistics and order fulfillment.
